#91 font-coverage correlation — 2026-11-18
AS3000 3.01 bitmap at 4EA60C equals Neo 3.21 bitmap at 44C4EE, all 32 bytes.
Thus both return false for the identical 73 inputs and true for 183 inputs,
consistent with the preceding all-256-input compiled MAME probes.
Default Neo font descriptor 44AF8E: 6x16, 12 bytes/glyph, bitmap 44A38E.
Among rejected inputs, 72 have ink; 00 is a generic filled block and A0 is blank.
NeoFontTerminal glyphs.png and glyphs.h match for all 256 glyphs. Its rejected
inputs have the same blank/fallback counts. Distinct glyph examples DD/DE/FD/FE
exist in both Neo fonts, while AS3000 3.01 maps those text bytes to FF fallback.
D0 and D7 are also rejected, despite non-FF AS LCD translations C2 and DB and
corresponding glyphs in the emulator CGROM asset. The routine is not a complete
renderability test on either platform. Legacy AS3000 3.01 text-repertoire/export
compatibility is a hypothesis, not a verified consumer contract.

marked NEEDS REDUMP; no physical glyph-shape certification is claimed.
